Why Aftergraph

Complete is not verified.

The system is designed around durable principles instead of confidence theatre.

Agent says done≠Verified outcome

Finished work still needs independent checking. Aftergraph separates self-report from proof at every layer.

1Claim — Agent reports completion
2Evidence — Inspectable artifacts emitted
3Independent verifier — Separate process checks criteria
4Verdict — Bound to exact subject, not self-report
