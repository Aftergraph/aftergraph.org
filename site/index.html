Thesis

From intent to verified outcome

An agent acts under a mission. It spends only what an authority allows. Every action leaves an evidence trail. A verifier can check the outcome. That is the operating model Aftergraph is built on.

Intent→Mission→Authority→Execution→Evidence→Verification→Verified outcome
